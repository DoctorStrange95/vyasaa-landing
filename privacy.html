Information We Collect from Users (Patients)

Vyasa is the sole owner of information collected on the Websites. We will not sell, share, transfer or rent any personal information to others in ways different from what is disclosed in this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

We collect information when you register on our platform or book an appointment. This information may include 'personal information' or sensitive personal data and information (SPI) as defined under the SPI Rules.

Personal information means any information that relates to a natural person which, directly or indirectly in combination with other available information, is capable of identifying that person.

Under the SPI Rules, sensitive personal data and information includes:

  1. Passwords
  2. Financial information such as bank accounts, credit/debit card details or other payment instrument details
  3. Physical, physiological and mental health conditions
  4. Sexual orientation
  5. Medical records and history
  6. Biometric information
  7. Information received under a lawful contract
  8. Visitor details provided at the time of registration
  9. Call data records

Information freely available in the public domain or accessible under the Right to Information Act, 2005 is not treated as personal or sensitive information.

Information We Collect from Doctors and Practitioners

Professional information includes but is not limited to your NMC registration number, medical council license number, qualifications, specialties, clinic details, availability schedule and consultation fees.

  • We may discontinue your access if any professional information you provide is found to be false or incorrect.
  • Upon approval, your professional profile (name, specialty, city, qualification, booking availability) will be listed publicly on our platform and may be accessible to registered and unregistered users.
  • You may add your clinic website link to your profile. These external links are not managed by us. Users visit them at their own risk.
  • Your patient records, visit notes and prescription data on Vyasa are stored securely and are accessible only to you and your authorised clinic staff.

Data Retention

Your personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, or as required by applicable law. We retain your data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide our services.

Once the purpose for which information was collected is achieved, personal data is deleted securely. After a period of time, data may be anonymised and aggregated and held by us as long as necessary to provide services effectively.

Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data held by Vyasa.

To submit a data deletion request:

  1. Send an email to support@vyasaa.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your registered email address or phone number in the body of the email so we can locate your account.

We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

Certain data may be retained after a deletion request where required by applicable law, regulation, or court order (for example, medical record retention mandated by healthcare legislation). Any retained data will be stored securely and used only for compliance purposes.
